ACME Technologies Pvt Ltd — Employee Handbook (Extract)
Hyderabad Office, Financial Year 2025-26

1. LEAVE POLICY

All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per financial
year. Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. A maximum of 12
unused days may be carried forward into the following financial year; anything
beyond that lapses on 31 March.

Casual leave is capped at 8 days per year and cannot be carried forward. Sick
leave is separate and capped at 12 days per year; absences longer than 3
consecutive days require a medical certificate.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in
advance, except in emergencies. Approval rests with the reporting manager.

2. WORKING HOURS AND REMOTE WORK

Core hours are 11:00 to 17:00 IST, during which all employees are expected to be
available. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible provided 8 hours per day are
worked.

Employees may work remotely up to 10 days per month. Fully remote arrangements
require director-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months.

3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of claim submission. Claims must
include original receipts and be filed within 60 days of the expense being
incurred. Claims filed after 60 days are not reimbursed.

Daily meal allowance during business travel is Rs. 1,200 for metro cities and
Rs. 800 for non-metro locations. Accommodation is capped at Rs. 6,000 per night.
Air travel must be booked in economy class unless the flight exceeds 6 hours.

4. PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation, either party
may terminate the employment with 15 days notice. After confirmation, the notice
period is 60 days for all employees below director level and 90 days at director
level and above.

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in April and October. Salary
revisions take effect from 1 July.

5. THIRD QUARTER REVENUE SUMMARY

Revenue for the third quarter of FY 2025-26 was Rs. 47.3 crore, an increase of
18 percent over the same quarter last year. The Hyderabad office contributed
Rs. 19.8 crore of that total. Operating margin improved from 12.4 percent to
14.1 percent.

The largest single contract signed during the quarter was with a banking client
in Mumbai, valued at Rs. 8.5 crore over three years.
